Context & Goals

LyricLoom is an innovative platform dedicated to co-creating song lyrics. Built for creative teams, it enables real-time collaboration, idea capture, and the editorial structuring of lyrics.

The project aimed to define a clear visual identity, guidelines for web and print use, and a motion direction that brings the brand voice to life across promotional and product content.

Approach & Process

The typographic approach focused on clear, modular reading: pairing a display face for titles with a contemporary sans-serif for interfaces ensures strong legibility while preserving visual character.

Weights and letter-spacing choices were documented in the brand book to ensure maximum consistency.

For motion design, clear principles were defined — tempo, easing, and micro-interactions — enabling content modules to animate without harming lyric legibility.

Animated assets were delivered as Lottie files and optimized sequences for web and social.

Results & Deliverables

The project delivered a complete identity: primary logo and variations, a brand book detailing usage, colors, and typography, an icon kit, editorial templates, and a series of product-specific animations.
